About This Game

Can you afford a single mistake?

Margin of Error is a roguelite dungeon RPG where every battle is a numbers puzzle and every answer counts.

Princess Elyra is bound to Tinta, a cursed grimoire with a sharp tongue and a long memory. To break the curse she must lead her party across six regions full of monsters and the generals who command them.

Each enemy carries a target number. Hit it exactly with your cards and your attack lands. Miss it, and the dungeon makes you pay.
